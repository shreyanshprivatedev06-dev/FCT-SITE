## 4. KPI formulas (time basis · denominator · exclusions)
- **P2P Health Score** (period): Σ(wᵢ·sᵢ)/Σ(wᵢ of components with data); sᵢ = clamp((value−floor)/(target−floor))×100. Weights/floor/target: approval 20/90/99, match 20/90/99.5, price 15/88/98, master data 15/85/99, GR 15/70/95, OTP 15/75/97. Missing component → excluded, coverage < 100% shown; never scored as perfect. Master-data quality has no history → Apr–Aug coverage 85%. Bands: Healthy ≥75, Watch 55–74, At risk <55.
- **Total Spend** (period): Σ posted invoice value − credit notes by received month. Excl. GST, intercompany, payroll, taxes, advances; freight and non-PO included.
- **Spend against budget** (period, group level): illustrative FY27 budget per category (`cats[].bud`, ₹ Cr a month, flat phasing). Period budget = monthly × months in period; YTD = Apr to the period's last month; FY remaining = 12 × monthly − YTD actual. Shown only with no BU, region, risk or vendor filter (the budget is not split below category). Σ category actual = Total Spend.
- **Open Commitments** (snapshot): Σ(PO line value − value received) for open lines. GRNI is a liability: shown beside, not added. Requisitions are not commitments. No budget comparison (no reliable budget feed).
- **Overdue Payables** (snapshot): Σ unpaid posted invoices with due < snapshot. Status split (Blocked, Disputed, Held, Approval pending, In run, Scheduled later) inside the total. Buckets 1–30/31–60/61–90/>90.
- **Potential Exposure** (snapshot): Σ estimate of cases with status Open or Under investigation and a financial method (one estimate per case). **Disputed** and **Confirmed loss** are separate measures; control cases (approval breach, no PO) show *value affected* beside, never added. Methods per type in `config.exposureTypes`.
- **Vendor Risk Mix** (snapshot): unique active vendors by *approved* category; spend share uses period spend (different denominator, toggle).
- **Top Vendors by Spend** (period): spend by stable vendor ID, top 10 + Others; share of Total Spend; sheet shows top-5 share.
- **Invoice Match Rate** (period, count-weighted; value-weighted beside): PO invoices matched first pass or after the cause was corrected ÷ PO invoices received. Forced (tolerance override) and open = not matched. Non-PO and credit notes excluded.
- **Approval Compliance** (period by PO release): PO lines approved at required DoA level before release ÷ PO lines released (invoiced or open). Breaches: approved after PO, approver below level, split, no approval.
- **Contract-Price Compliance** (period): contracted lines priced within a valid contract ±2% ÷ contracted lines. Expired contract = non-compliant; renewal-not-loaded = compliant (PO master-data issue, own exposure case); UoM lines excluded pending unit check; no-contract lines excluded (coverage shown).
- **Goods Receipt Timeliness** (period by gate entry): receipts posted ≤48 h after gate entry ÷ receipts with a gate time; unposted > 48 h = late; < 48 h unposted excluded. Sites without a gate feed (IN-06, IN-01 HQ, US-01, EU-01) = N/A, coverage shown.
- **On-Time Payment Rate** (period by payment date): paid on/before due date (calendar day) ÷ invoices paid. Unpaid overdue reported separately.
- **Master-data quality** (supporting): passed ÷ (active vendors × 6 checks).

## 5. Vendor risk (trailing six months; independent of the period filter)
Factors 0–100: financial distress (master), delivery and quality ((95−OTIF)×6 + (rejects−1)×12), price (vendor non-compliance rate ÷ population rate × 25; FX tolerance excluded), control (exception rate ÷ population rate × 30), master data and due diligence (failed checks/6×200 + insolvency 50 + GST 25 + KYC 40), dependency (sole source 70 + category share×150 ≤30), payment fraud (pending bank change 100, SoD-created 70). **Score = 0.6 × worst factor + 0.4 × weighted average** of assessed factors (factors with < 5 records are N/A and lower coverage). Escalation floors: insolvency 85, unverified bank change 88, cancelled GST 80, SoD-created vendor 65. Model category vs approved category → "re-review proposed" (Apex, Sai Enterprises, Coastal Packaging).

## 6. Process chain
Issue codes (`config.issues` + model extras) map each break to a stage. Stage totals count a record once per stage; issue rows count each issue. Requisition-to-invoice stages use invoices received in the period plus snapshot open items (open PO lines, open requisitions, GRNI > 90 days); the payment stage uses payments made in the period plus unpaid overdue. Value affected = record value (not exposure).

## 7. Interaction and state
State: selected KPI, global filters (period, BU, region, category, risk, vendor; sessionStorage per viewer), breadcrumb path (`M.nav`, mirrored in the URL hash; `desk.html#kpi=otp` deep-links), local selection per node, current vendor (from path, else filter), case status overrides (`FCT.state.notes.p2pCase`), drawer decisions (shared `FCT.state`). Single click selects and updates local detail; double click, Enter, or the › button drills. Crumbs restore that node and its saved selection. KPI switch resets the path, keeps filters, and confirms if an investigation note is unsaved. Loading = React transition indicator; errors = boundary that shows no partial numbers; empty = honest "no records" with reset.

## 8. Assumptions and missing backend fields
Assumptions to confirm: DoA thresholds (₹10 L / ₹1 Cr / ₹5 Cr), 48 h GR SLA, ±2% price tolerance, health weights/floors/targets, risk weights and escalation floors, MSME interest at 19.5% p.a., exposure methods. Backend fields needed for a real integration: requisition submit/approve timestamps and approver authority; PO release timestamp; gate-entry timestamps per site; contract price list with validity and UoM; invoice IRN, capture confidence and match outcome history; payment run and bank-change audit log; vendor credit/MCA/GST feeds; KYC and sanctions screening dates (shown as missing, not passed); budget feed (the category budget is illustrative; commitment vs budget not shown).
