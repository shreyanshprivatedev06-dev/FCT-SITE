# SC control tower — calculation model

Every number on `screens/sc/` is calculated by `assets/js/sc/sc-model.js` (`window.SCM`) from the seeded ledger in `data/sc/ledger.js`. Configuration, material-plants and suppliers live in `data/sc/master.js` (`FCT_DATA.sc3`). Shared universe anchors (book inventory, receipts not booked, named exceptions) stay in `data/sc.js`, `data/exceptions.js` and `data/sc/anomalies.js` and must still reconcile. Demand records come from `data/sc/demand.js` (generated by `tools/sc/gen-demand.js`). Tests: `node tools/sc/test-model.js [-v]`.

## Screens (same probing model as P2P and O2C)
- **Control tower** (`desk.html`): KPI sheet → in-place KPI detail under a global breadcrumb. Deep link `desk.html#kpi=<id>`.
- **Material profiles** (`materials.html`): portfolio → Material Profile → document / case. Supplier is a dimension (supplier dependence table, supplier breakdowns), not a screen.
- **Process chain** (`process.html`): Forecast → Plan → Procure → Receive → Produce → Ship, with the root causes (RC-01…RC-12) mapped to the stage they break.

The main entity is a **material at a plant** (`KSM-17@IN-02A`). Internal identifiers keep `vendor` (filter key, fact dimension) so the shared UI stays close to P2P; visible copy always says **material**.

The old nine pages fold in as: valuation, cost, working capital and integrity → KPIs on the sheet (Inventory Value, Excess Stock, Valuation Review, Record Accuracy); supplier risk → Material Profiles; demand, receipts and root causes → Process chain stages and issue types.

## KPIs
Sheet: Health, Inventory Value, Excess Stock, Receipts Not Booked, Material Exposure, Material Risk Mix, Top Materials by Value, and the control rates Forecast Accuracy (1 − WMAPE, bias beside it), Inventory Record Accuracy, Receipt Timeliness, Valuation Review Completeness, Supplier OTIF. Support: material master-data quality. DIO and turns (365 ÷ DIO) come from the finance metric store (`sc.dio`). Period flows and snapshot balances are labelled separately.

**Health** = weighted normalised score of six control-tower themes: demand planning & supply assurance 15, procurement & sourcing 15, supplier performance & risk 20, inventory & material availability 20, production, warehousing & logistics 15, controls, cost & data integrity 15. Each theme is the average of its subtheme pass rates (`config.themes`); each subtheme is a pass/fail check on records, so the drill runs theme → subtheme → plant / class / supplier → material → record. Snapshot subthemes exist only at the book date, so earlier months are not scored and the prior-period comparison is shown only like for like. Sep-26: 54, Watch.

**Segmentation** on the Health card is ABC (stock value, 80/95/100% cumulative) × XYZ (consumption CoV <0.25 / <0.5 / ≥0.5). A chip re-scopes only the component tiles and compares that slice with all materials. Class is not inventory risk; risk is a separate six-factor score (cover, ageing/expiry, supply, record accuracy, valuation, forecast error).

## Cases and honesty
Each of the 40 named SC exceptions has exactly one case (`CS-…`), linked to its drawer exception, so **Decide** stays in the shared drawer and the case status follows it.
- Financial cases carry one estimate = confirmed + estimated parts from `D.scx.meta`. **Material Exposure** = Σ estimates of open financial cases (₹85.79 Cr at 09:10). It replaces the old desk's ₹88.86 Cr, which counted High-risk anomalies from all towers.
- Contingent parts (revenue at risk, slow-moving on watch, input credit at risk) are reported beside, never added.
- Control cases (excess, stock statement, no-PO, adjustment authority, stale plan, process breaches) show value affected, never a loss.
- Unquantified cases show N/A, never ₹0.

Decisions move the numbers. For example, deciding SC-GIT-06 books the 19 shipments: Receipts Not Booked falls from 19.0 to 6.4 and Inventory rises to 654.6.
